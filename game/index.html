SOLARA BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

Mara Voss came home to Solara Bay with nothing but a debt and a fast right foot. Steal cars, outrun the Bay Police, run jobs for fixer Delia Crane, and buy your way out — one score at a time. The whole city is yours: drive it, walk it, take it.

WASD move / drive  ·  Mouse aim  ·  Click shoot  ·  E enter / exit car  ·  SHIFT sprint  ·  SPACE handbrake  ·  P pause  ·  M mute

On foot: WASD walks, mouse turns the camera, click fires your pistol. Near a car press E to steal it — the owner may call the cops. In a car: W accelerates, S brakes, A/D steer, SPACE handbrakes. Wanted stars rise when the police see you commit crimes; hide or outrun them to cool off. Gold markers are missions — follow the minimap. Your progress saves automatically.
